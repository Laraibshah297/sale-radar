Where prices come from

  • Retailer feeds: stores share a CSV file, Google Sheet or product feed with us, under a data agreement.
  • Manual checks: our team adds or rechecks offers on the retailer's website.
  • Permitted automatic collection: only where a retailer has given written permission, at a low frequency.

We do not bypass logins, CAPTCHAs, paywalls or technical blocks, and we stop collecting from a store if it asks us to.

Freshness

Every offer shows “last checked”. Prices older than {{ settings.STALE_HOURS }} hours are marked Stale price, and prices older than {{ (settings.HIDE_HOURS / 24)|int }} days are hidden.

How offers are ranked

By default: lowest known delivered price (item + shipping) first. Offers where shipping is unknown come after, sorted by item price, and are labelled “Unknown”. In-stock offers come before out-of-stock ones. Ranking is never changed by advertising or commission. Paid placements appear in a separate box labelled Sponsored.

Matching products

We only compare offers for exactly the same model and variant (storage, RAM, colour, region such as PTA-approved, and condition). We use barcodes (GTIN) and model numbers first. Uncertain matches are checked by a person before they are shown.

Deal labels

“Lowest price in 30 days” and “Below the usual price” compare today's price with prices we actually observed, not with the store's crossed-out price.

See something wrong?

Use “Report incorrect price” next to any offer. We review reports promptly.
